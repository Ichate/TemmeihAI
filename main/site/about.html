* about *

ai bots that actually play minecraft with you.

not just stand there. not just follow you around. actually play like fight, build, craft, cook, grind, talk. like a real player, but powered by an AI.

talks - real conversations in chat

walks - follows you, navigates on its own

fights - alongside you or against you

crafts - figures out recipes for you

cooks - so you stop dying of hunger

grinds - farms while you do other stuff

builds - describe something and watch it happen
